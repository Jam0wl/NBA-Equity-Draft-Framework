NBA Equity Draft Framework (EDF)

The NBA Equity Draft Framework (EDF) is a structural reform proposal for the NBA First Round Draft. It aims to minimize tanking incentives while preserving competitive balance and trade flexibility.

Core Principles

  • Base as structural balance mechanism
  • Equity as performance-based end-of-season currency
  • Full 1–14 weighted lottery draw
  • Deterministic playoff ordering with dampened equity
  • Strict pool-bound application of equity
